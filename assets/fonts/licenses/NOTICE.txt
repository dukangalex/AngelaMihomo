for each font bundled under `assets/fonts/`:

|---|---|---|---|---|

| `Twemoji.Mozilla.ttf` | Twemoji COLRv0 | Twitter, Inc and other contributors (artwork); Mozilla Foundation (build code) | CC BY 4.0 (artwork); Apache-2.0 (code) | `Twemoji-Attribution.txt` |
| `Icons.ttf` | Icons (single glyph) | Keyamoon / IcoMoon | CC BY 4.0 attribution basis | `Icons-IcoMoon-Attribution.txt` |
| `NotoSansSC-Regular.ttf` | Noto Sans SC 2.004 (subset) | Google / Adobe (Noto CJK) | SIL Open Font License 1.1 | `NotoSansSC-OFL.txt` |
