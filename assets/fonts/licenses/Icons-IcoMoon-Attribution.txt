Attribution for Icons.ttf

The bundled file assets/fonts/Icons.ttf is a single-glyph icon font
(a "target" pictogram, U+E900) generated with the IcoMoon app
(https://icomoon.io), as recorded in the font's own metadata
("Font generated by IcoMoon.").

Per IcoMoon's published terms, fonts generated from one's own vectors
carry no license requirements. To the extent the glyph derives from the
IcoMoon Free icon pack by Keyamoon, that pack is dual-licensed under the
GPL and Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0); this
project relies on the CC BY 4.0 option and attributes as follows:

    Icons by Keyamoon — IcoMoon (https://icomoon.io)
    Licensed under CC BY 4.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
